# Teacher accounts

Administrators decide who may create courses. Administrators are teachers
too: everything in the [teacher pages](./teachers/quick-start.md) applies.

## Approve teacher applications

Someone who signs up as a teacher without a signup link becomes an
*application*. You get an email for each one.

1. On the course list, click **🛡️ Admin**.
2. Under **Teacher applications** you see name and email of each applicant.
3. Click **Approve** to make them a teacher — they get an email — or
   **Reject** to delete the account.

## Teacher signup links

To skip the approval step for colleagues you already know, send them the
teacher signup link. Anyone who creates an account through it becomes a
teacher immediately, so share it personally and do not post it publicly.

The link is kept by whoever maintains the server; ask them if you do not have
it.
