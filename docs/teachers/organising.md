# Organising a course

## Course details

In **⚙️ Course settings** → **General**:

- **Name** and **Subtitle** — shown on the course list and the printed cover.
- **Language** — the language of the content. It is passed to the AI prompt and
  sets the language of the app's buttons inside the course.
- **Chapter numbering** — see below.

Only the **owner** of a course can change its settings; see
[co-editors](./sharing.md#co-editors).

## Chapter order

Drag a chapter in the chapter list by its handle **⠿** to move it. You can drag
it within a part or into another part.

## Parts

Parts group chapters by topic, for example *Formalism* and *Applications*.

1. **⚙️ Course settings** → **Parts**: type a name and click **Add part**.
2. Put chapters in a part by dragging them in the chapter list, or with the
   **Part** field in the chapter editor.

Parts can be renamed, moved up or down, and dragged in the chapter list.
Deleting a part keeps its chapters; they move to the top of the list.
Chapters without a part are listed above the parts.

## Chapter numbering

- **Continuous (1, 2, 3…)** — chapters are numbered through the whole course.
- **Per part (1.1, 1.2, 2.1…)** — numbering restarts in each part.

This only changes how numbers are displayed (also in print). It does not
change the order or the chapter numbers used for [import](./ai-import.md).

## Delete a course

**⚙️ Course settings** → **Danger zone** → **🗑️ Delete course**. This
permanently deletes the course and all its chapters. Export the chapters you
want to keep first.
