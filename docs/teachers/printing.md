# Printing and PDF

Any course can be printed or saved as a PDF — by you and by your students.

## Make a PDF

1. Click **🖨️ Print / PDF** at the bottom of the chapter list.
2. **What do you want to print?** This chapter, one part, or the whole course.
3. **Include:**
   - **Formulas:** at the end of each chapter, collected at the back as one
     formula sheet, or left out.
   - **Exercises (with hints and solutions)** — off by default.
   - Quizzes are never printed.
4. Click **Open preview**. Wait until it says **Ready to print** (figures and
   formulas are loading).
5. Click **🖨️ Print** and choose *Save as PDF* as the printer.

## Book layout

**📖 Book layout** turns the preview into real pages with running heads, page
numbers and a table of contents with page numbers.

- Wait until **Paginating…** is gone before you print. Printing earlier gives a
  half-finished document.
- In the browser's print dialog, turn off **Headers and footers**; the book
  layout has its own.
- It works best in **Chrome** or **Edge**. If it fails, the app says so and
  prints the plain layout instead.

## Missing figures

If figures have not been uploaded yet, teachers see a warning in the preview
listing them. They are left out of the print — upload them first (see
[Figures](./writing.md#figures)).
