# Accounts and signing in

MiniLectures runs in your browser at [minilectures.app](https://minilectures.app).
There is nothing to install.

## Do I need an account?

- **To read a public course:** no. Courses marked as public can be opened by
  anyone from the course list.
- **To join a course with an invite code, keep your quiz progress, or teach:**
  yes.

## Create an account

1. Click **Sign in** (top right), then **Create account**.
2. Fill in your name, email address and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Choose the **Account type**:
   - **Student** — to follow courses.
   - **Teacher** — to create courses. See [teacher accounts](#teacher-accounts)
     below.
4. Click **Create account**.

You get an email asking you to confirm your address. Click the button in it;
the app opens and tells you the address is confirmed. Then sign in.

::: tip No email?
Check your spam folder. The email comes from `noreply@minilectures.app`.
:::

### Teacher accounts

- **With a teacher signup link** from the administrator, your account is a
  teacher account straight away.
- **Without one**, your application waits for the administrator. They get an
  email; you get one as soon as you are approved. Until then you can sign in,
  but not create courses.

## Sign in

Click **Sign in**, enter your email address and password. Capital letters in
the email address do not matter.

## Forgot your password?

1. Click **Sign in** → **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address and click **Send reset email**.
3. Open the email and click **Choose a new password**. The app opens with the
   reset form already filled in.
4. Enter your new password twice and click **Set new password**, then sign in.

The link works for a limited time. If it has expired, simply request a new one.

## Language

The course list is in English. Inside a course, buttons and menus follow the
language of that course (English or Dutch).
