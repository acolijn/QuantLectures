# Using a course

## Join a course

Your teacher gives you an **invite code** (for example `QM2026`) or a link.

- **With a link:** open it. If you are not signed in yet, sign in or create an
  account first; the code is then filled in for you.
- **With a code:** sign in, click **Add a course** on the course list, type the
  code and click **Join**.

The course now appears in your course list. Public courses are already listed
and need no code.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *This invite code does not exist or has been revoked.* | Check the spelling, or ask your teacher for a new code. |
| *This invite code has reached its maximum number of uses.* | Ask your teacher to raise the limit or make a new code. |
| *You are already enrolled in this course.* | Nothing — it is in your course list already. |

## Find your way around

Open a course from the course list. On the left is the list of chapters,
sometimes grouped into parts. **← All courses** takes you back to the list.

Each chapter has tabs:

- **📖 Concepts** — the explanation, with formulas.
- **📐 Formulas** — the key formulas of the chapter on one page.
- **🧮 Exercises** — guided exercises, step by step.
- **🖼️ Figures** — the chapter's figures (also shown in the text).
- **✏️ Quiz** — multiple-choice questions to test yourself.

## Quizzes

Answer each question; you see straight away whether you were right, with an
explanation. At the end you get your score. Your **best score** per chapter is
saved and shown next to the chapter in the list, and counts towards your
progress (**Progress: done/total** at the bottom of the chapter list).

You can redo a quiz as often as you like. To start over completely, use
**Reset progress**.

## Exercises

Exercises guide you through a problem in steps.

1. Read the step, work it out on paper, and type your answer in the box.
2. Click **Check**. Numbers are accepted with a small tolerance (usually 1%),
   so rounding is fine. Units may be added (`9.8 m/s^2`), and `x = 42` counts
   the same as `42`.
3. Stuck? Open a **Hint**, or **▸ Show solution** for the worked answer.
4. **Next step →** moves on.

Some steps have no automatic check. They show a box for **Your working**:
write it down there and compare with the solution yourself.

::: tip Typing formulas
Answers can be plain numbers or simple LaTeX, such as `\frac{1}{2}` or
`2\pi`.
:::

## Print or save as PDF

Click **🖨️ Print / PDF** at the bottom of the chapter list. Choose what to
print (this chapter, one part, or the whole course) and whether to include
formulas and exercises, then click **Open preview** and **🖨️ Print**. In the
print dialog of your browser, choose *Save as PDF* to get a file.

Quizzes are never printed. More details: [Printing and PDF](../teachers/printing.md).
